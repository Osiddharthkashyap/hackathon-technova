# Data Analysis Skill

## Inspect first
Determine schema, row counts, column types, missingness, duplicates, units, date ranges, and likely keys.

## Preserve semantics
Do not silently coerce IDs, leading-zero codes, dates, currencies, or categorical labels. Track unit conversions explicitly.

## Calculations
Define formulas before execution. Reconcile totals to source data. Check for divide-by-zero, overflow, outliers, duplicate joins, and accidental filtering.

## Output
Include definitions, assumptions, and scope for important metrics. Validate generated spreadsheets and charts.
