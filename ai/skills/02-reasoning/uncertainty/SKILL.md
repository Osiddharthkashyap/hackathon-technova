# Uncertainty Skill

## Rules
- Separate observation from inference.
- Never turn an estimate into a fact.
- Identify the source of uncertainty: missing input, stale source, conflicting evidence, model limitation, or measurement error.
- State the practical consequence when uncertainty matters.

## Confidence discipline
Use qualitative labels only when they clarify evidence quality; do not invent numeric confidence scores without a valid method.

## Conflict resolution
Prefer direct, authoritative, recent, and independently corroborated evidence. Preserve meaningful disagreement and attribute contested interpretations.

## User-facing language
Use concise formulations such as “verified,” “calculated,” “assumed,” “not verified,” and “estimate” when needed.
