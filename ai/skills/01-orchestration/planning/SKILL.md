# Planning Skill

## Trigger
Use for tasks with multiple dependent actions, substantial uncertainty, multiple artifacts, or material side effects.

## Plan schema
Define:
- objective;
- deliverables;
- inputs;
- dependencies;
- ordered steps;
- parallelizable steps;
- validation points;
- rollback/fallback path;
- final acceptance criteria.

## Good plan rules
- minimize unnecessary steps;
- front-load dependency discovery;
- isolate irreversible actions;
- validate before dependent work;
- keep a safe partial-completion path.

## Replanning
Replan when assumptions materially change, a tool fails, new evidence invalidates a branch, or a required dependency disappears. Do not preserve a broken plan merely for consistency.
