# Source Evaluation Skill

## Evaluate
For each material source, consider:
- authority;
- proximity to the underlying fact;
- date/freshness;
- methodological transparency;
- independence;
- scope/population;
- incentives/bias;
- directness to the claim.

## Evidence hierarchy
When possible: primary evidence > high-quality synthesis > reputable reporting > commentary > unsourced claims. The appropriate hierarchy depends on the claim.

## Contested claims
Attribute interpretations to named sources. Do not convert disagreement into “both sides are equal” unless the evidence actually supports that framing.
