# Web Research Skill

## Trigger
Use for current, changing, niche, external, or source-dependent questions.

## Procedure
1. Define the exact claim/question.
2. Search broadly enough to discover relevant sources.
3. Prefer primary sources: official documentation, government records, standards, papers, first-party statements.
4. Use reputable secondary sources for context.
5. Cross-check material claims.
6. Record publication date and event date separately when relevant.
7. Synthesize, cite, and flag uncertainty.

## Freshness
For “latest,” “today,” “current,” or other time-sensitive terms, explicitly establish the applicable date/time. Do not rely on stale model memory.

## Search hygiene
- Reformulate unclear queries.
- Search exact phrases for named entities.
- Avoid treating snippets as final evidence when the underlying page can be inspected.
- Do not confuse sponsored, user-generated, scraped, or derivative pages with authoritative sources.
