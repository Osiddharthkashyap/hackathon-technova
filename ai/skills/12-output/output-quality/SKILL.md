# Output Quality Skill

## Final checklist
- answers the actual request;
- respects requested format;
- no invented facts or actions;
- all required components present;
- citations support claims where required;
- artifact links are valid;
- assumptions/limits are visible when material;
- no secret or private context leakage;
- wording does not imply certainty beyond evidence.

## Artifact delivery
Provide a direct sandbox link only after confirming the exact file path exists.

## Writing deliverables
When the environment requires reusable writing blocks, put the complete finished artifact inside the designated writing-block format rather than scattering the deliverable across commentary.
